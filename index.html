Privacy Policy

Stud Detector & Wall Scanner

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fieldscan (“the App”) uses device sensors and handles information while providing magnetic-field detection, stud-finding assistance, compass functions, and scan history. This policy describes the current locally processed features; third-party advertising or analytics integrations must be disclosed separately if added.

1. Information the App Uses

  • Magnetometer readings: The App accesses magnetic-field sensor values to show live readings, detection alerts, calibration, graphs, and scan summaries.
  • Accelerometer readings: The App uses device orientation information to support the compass.
  • Location: If you enable True North and grant location permission, the App uses device location to calculate magnetic declination and adjust the compass heading. Location is not required for basic metal detection.
  • Preferences: The App saves choices such as language, appearance, alert settings, and detection sensitivity on your device.
  • Scan history: If you save scans, the App stores scan details such as time, duration, detection mode, and peak measurements locally on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

The App uses this information to display sensor measurements, support compass calculations, provide detection feedback, remember your preferences, and display saved scan history.

3. Local Storage and Data Sharing

The current App implementation stores settings and scan history locally on the device. The App's own sensor and scan-history features do not require an account or a remote server. We do not intentionally transmit scan history or raw magnetic sensor readings to a developer-operated server.

Android and device-level services used to obtain a location may operate according to your device provider's separate privacy terms. If third-party SDKs are introduced, this policy must be updated to accurately explain their data practices.

4. Permissions

  • Approximate or precise location (if granted): Used for True North correction. You may deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings; the compass may then display Magnetic North instead.
  • Vibration: Used to provide optional detection alerts.

The App does not need location access to read the magnetometer for metal detection.

5. Advertising and Analytics

Before publication: This section must match the final release. If you integrate CAS, AdMob, analytics, crash reporting, or another third-party SDK, add the providers, links to their privacy policies, data categories, and any consent choices that apply. Do not publish this text unchanged if such SDKs are present.

No advertising or analytics provider is represented as active by this policy.

6. Retention and Deletion

Saved settings and scan history remain on your device until removed through available App controls, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App (subject to Android backup and restore behavior). You can delete saved scans using the History screen. You can also revoke location permission in Android Settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is a utility tool and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information through an account system. Any third-party services added later may have separate requirements.

8. Security

The App relies on Android's application storage protections for locally saved information. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Important Safety Notice

Fieldscan measures magnetic disturbances. It cannot reliably identify wooden studs, live electrical wiring, plastic pipes, or safe drilling locations. Do not rely on the App for construction or electrical safety decisions.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, permissions, data practices, or third-party services change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published.
