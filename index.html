Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Stud Detector – Metal Scanner

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stud Detector – Metal Scanner (the “App”, “we”, “our”) handles information when you use the App. The Android application identifier is com.wcs.stud.detector.finder.metal.scanner.nail.

1. Information and device sensors

The App uses compatible device sensors, including the magnetometer and orientation-related sensors, to display magnetic-field measurements, detection indicators, and compass information. Sensor measurements are processed to provide the App's features. We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

The App may save scan history and preferences on your device. This can include recorded measurement values, scan timestamps, language and display settings, and other feature preferences. This locally stored information is not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server by these features.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The App integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, device information, app interactions, and ad performance data, subject to applicable permissions, consent choices, and Google's policies. This data may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization.

For details, see Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy guidance.

3. How information is used

  • To provide metal/stud detection and compass-related functionality.
  • To store scan history and user-selected settings locally.
  • To maintain app functionality and troubleshoot issues.
  • To display, measure, and protect advertising through AdMob.

4. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and enabled features, the App may use network access to load advertisements and device vibration for alerts or feedback. It may access device sensors to provide detection and compass functionality. The presence of network access does not mean your local scan history is sent to our servers.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your locally stored scan history. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google and its partners as described above. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law.

6. Data retention and deletion

Scan history and settings are stored on your device until removed using available App controls, by clearing the App's storage, or by uninstalling the App, subject to Android backup/restore settings. Google controls retention of data processed by its advertising services under its own policies. You may manage advertising privacy choices in Android settings and applicable consent prompts.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary collection and rely on Android's app storage protections for locally stored data. No system or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may process information subject to their policies and applicable child-protection requirements.

9. International processing

Third-party advertising providers may process data in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy policies and applicable legal safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, its integrations, or legal requirements. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions, data-related requests, or support, contact us at wolfclawsstudio@gmail.com.

Important: Sensor-based detection is indicative only. It may not reliably locate hidden wiring, pipes, studs, or hazardous materials. Do not rely solely on the App for safety-critical drilling or construction decisions.

This policy describes the reviewed project features and AdMob integration. Review it again if you add analytics, cloud synchronization, account creation, or additional SDKs.
